How a round works

1

News & Analysis · 60 s

A headline appears with an explanation and the industries involved. Players think about who wins and who loses. Future price moves stay hidden.

2

Trading · 60 s

Prices are frozen. Buy or sell any of the 24 stocks in euro amounts, fractional shares allowed. Tap I'm Ready to finish early.

3

Market Reaction · 120 s

The server reveals the simulated price moves, explains why each stock moved and updates the leaderboard.

The host assigns every player's starting budget, can pause, run the clock at 1×/2×/4×, and skip phases. All timing and money logic lives on the server.

Rules at a glance

💶 Capital-gains tax (in-game)

Sold at a profit, held under 4 rounds28%
Held 4 or more full rounds20%
Sold at a loss0%, no refund

Lots are sold first-in-first-out and taxed one by one. Game mechanics only: not tax advice.

⚡ Power-ups after round 6 (€5,000 each, max one)

🔮 The Oracle: privately learn if one stock goes UP, DOWN or FLAT.

🛡️ Crash Shield: reimburses market losses up to €10,000.

🚀 Profit Rocket: bonus of 2× a stock's gain on up to €50,000 (max €15,000).

Cada um é uma história fictícia que ensina uma ideia de finanças. Cada empresa tem ainda um movimento aleatório por ronda (até ±5% nas calmas e ±15% nas mais voláteis), por isso nada é totalmente previsível.

Honest data labelling

Company logos are fetched from public favicon services. Starting prices in demo mode are simulated reference values and are labelled as such. Headlines in the practice scenarios are fictional; an optional GDELT integration adds clearly separated, linked real-world coverage. Stock reactions are always simulated.

Under the hood

Server-authoritative engine

  • Node.js game server, zero-framework HTTP API with polling sync
  • Phase state machine with a server clock, pause and 1×/2×/4× speed
  • Money in integer cents, shares in micro-units: no floating-point drift
  • FIFO tax lots, idempotent orders and one-time round settlement
  • Seeded PRNG market built from market + sector + news + noise
  • Hashed player and host tokens; future returns never leave the server

Quality

  • Automated tests for tax, FIFO, power-ups, readiness, pause, privacy and a full HTTP game
  • 1,000-game strategy simulation to balance volatility and power-ups
  • Mobile-first UI, reduced-motion support, optional sound and haptics
  • GitHub Actions: tests on every push, site deployed to GitHub Pages
